Drawabox's 50% rule says that for every stretch of practice you should give yourself equal time to do something fun. This clock keeps me honest about it: time on Study pushes the balance up, time on Leisure spends it down, and a meter shows where I stand. Daily totals reset at midnight while the balance carries over, and state persists across reloads.
