Like the scratch-off travel posters, but on the web. Every country starts covered in gold foil; click one to scratch it away and reveal it in colour. The US and Canada are split into their states and provinces, and every US and Canadian national park is a dot you can mark too. Marking a park scratches off the state it is in. Flags and park photos light up as the counters climb, and your progress is saved in your browser.
